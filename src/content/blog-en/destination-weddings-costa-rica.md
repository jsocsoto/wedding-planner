Costa Rica is not just a destination — it is a living canvas. Volcanic peaks, turquoise coastlines, tropical forests, and colonial architecture create a backdrop so extraordinary that no floral arrangement could ever compete. For couples dreaming of a wedding that feels cinematic and deeply personal, this small country delivers on every level.

Here are five venues that have left our couples — and us — completely breathless.

## 1. Westfalia, Limón — The Caribbean Soul

The Caribbean coast of Costa Rica carries a rhythm entirely its own. The colors are brighter, the music warmer, the air thicker with the scent of the sea. Westfalia offers intimate properties nestled between the jungle and the beach, where the sound of howler monkeys mingles with the sound of waves. Perfect for couples seeking something raw, authentic, and unforgettable.

## 2. Escazú, San José — Urban Elegance

Not every destination wedding needs to be beachside. Escazú, nestled in the hills above San José, offers haciendas and boutique venues with sweeping valley views, cool mountain air, and a refined atmosphere that pairs beautifully with candlelit receptions and live jazz.

## 3. Puerto Viejo, Limón — Bohemian Paradise

Puerto Viejo carries a free spirit that attracts couples who want their wedding to feel like an adventure. Think barefoot ceremonies on volcanic sand beaches, open-air receptions under fairy lights strung between palm trees, and sunsets that defy description.

## 4. Orosí, Cartago — Sacred History

The Orosí Valley is one of Costa Rica's best-kept secrets. Surrounded by coffee plantations and colonial churches, it offers a profound sense of history and tranquility. The valley's lush green hills and thermal rivers create a setting unlike any other — ideal for couples who want their wedding to feel timeless.

## 5. San Pablo, Heredia — Intimate Garden Weddings

For the couple who dreams of a garden wedding surrounded by tropical blooms and mountain views, the coffee-growing region around Heredia offers elegant private estates that feel worlds away from the ordinary.
