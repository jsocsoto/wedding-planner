There was a time when eloping meant rushing to a courthouse and keeping a secret. That time is long gone. Today's couples are discovering something far more powerful: a luxury elopement is not a compromise — it is an elevation.

When you strip away the seating charts, the distant relatives, the speeches that run long, what remains is the essential thing. You. Each other. The vows you actually want to say.

## What Makes an Elopement "Luxury"

It is not about cost. It is about intention. A luxury elopement means that every element — the florals, the music, the setting, the photographer, the champagne — has been chosen with care, not with compromise. Nothing is there because "that's how it's done." Everything is there because you chose it.

At Kimaura Allen Events, we design elopements that feel like the opening scene of a film you will watch for the rest of your lives.

## Our Favorite Elopement Settings in Costa Rica

**The Orosí Valley** is perhaps our most intimate setting. Mist clings to the hills each morning. The silence is interrupted only by birdsong and the sound of the thermal river below. We have helped couples exchange vows in the valley at sunrise — with just a photographer, an officiant, and a table set for two with the finest local cuisine.

**Westfalia, Limón** offers something different: the Caribbean coastline at its most raw and beautiful. Ceremonies at the water's edge, where the jungle meets the sea, and a reception on a private terrace as the stars emerge. There is nothing quite like it.

**Private estates in Escazú** bring urban elegance to the table. Rooftop ceremonies above the city lights, with the Central Valley spread out below like a map of everything still to come.

## The Couple This Is For

The luxury elopement is for the couple who knows exactly what they want and has no interest in performing it for an audience. It is for the partners who would rather spend their wedding budget on a month in Europe than a banquet hall. It is for the people who look at each other and say: *let's just go.*

If that sounds like you, we would love to hear your story.
